My Journey

Technical Consulting Software, AI & Data

I joined Leyton as a Scientific Consultant, where I developed extensive experience analysing software, AI, data and engineering projects across a diverse portfolio of organisations. My work combined technical investigation, client-facing workshops, analytical reporting and technology assessment.

I was subsequently promoted to Technical Consultant, taking greater ownership of technically complex software, AI and data-intensive engagements. Today, I lead technical analysis and scoping activities, evaluating system architectures, data pipelines, machine learning components, scalability constraints and technical performance while working directly with engineering teams and senior stakeholders.

Master’s in Collective Intelligence UM6P

I completed a Master’s degree in Collective Intelligence at the School of Collective Intelligence, Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P). The interdisciplinary programme combined artificial intelligence, cognitive science, data science and social systems analysis, exploring how humans, machines and organisations generate knowledge and make decisions.

My work included experimental design, statistical modelling, forecasting, belief updating, trust and credibility, participatory systems and quantitative evaluation. This experience developed my interest in the relationship between AI, human decision-making and intelligent systems.

Industry AI Research Nokia

My Master’s thesis was conducted in collaboration with Nokia and focused on deep learning for industrial image classification. I evaluated CNN architectures and ensemble learning approaches under real-world constraints including class imbalance, limited labelled data and computational cost. The research strengthened my experience in machine learning experimentation, model evaluation and computer vision. • Explore my thesis →
